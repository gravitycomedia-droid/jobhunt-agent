# Delete your FirstRole account and data

FirstRole is the AI job-search agent app (developer: gravity.co.media@gmail.com
on Google Play). This page explains how to delete your account and what
happens to your data when you do — whether or not you currently have the app
installed.

## Option 1: Delete in-app (instant)

1. Open FirstRole and sign in.
2. Go to **Profile**.
3. Tap **Delete account**.
4. On the confirmation screen, press and hold **"Hold to delete permanently"**
   for about a second.

Your account and data are deleted immediately — there is no waiting period.
You are signed out automatically once it completes.

## Option 2: Request deletion without the app

If you no longer have the app installed or can't sign in, email
**gravity.co.media@gmail.com** from the email address associated with your
account and ask for it to be deleted. We will process it within 30 days and
confirm by email when it's done.

## What gets deleted

Deleting your account permanently removes:

- Your profile (name, email, account info)
- Your uploaded resume and the structured profile extracted from it
- Job matches and match scores
- Tailored resumes generated for specific jobs
- Application tracker entries and notes
- Notifications, follow-up drafts, and chat/message history
- Your sign-in credentials (you cannot log back into the same account)

This is permanent and cannot be undone.

## What is retained

Anonymized operational logs of AI calls (which model ran, token counts,
latency, and pass/fail validation) are kept for cost and reliability
monitoring after deletion, but with the link to your account removed —
they cannot be traced back to you. These logs store only a cryptographic
hash of the prompt, never resume content or personal data.

See the full [privacy policy](privacy-policy) for more detail on data handling.
